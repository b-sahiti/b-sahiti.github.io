Spire — Co-creator

Open-source intrusion-tolerant SCADA and substation-protection system engineered to withstand system- and network-level compromise while meeting power-grid timing requirements, including 100–200 ms control-center updates and 4.167 ms autonomous substation-protection actions.

  • Spire 1.3: Optimized transaction latency by approximately 3× and integrated machine-learning-based network intrusion detection.
  • Spire 2.0: Developed the first real-time Byzantine-resilient protection system for power-grid substations; delivered it for deployment at Pacific Northwest National Laboratory, Siemens, GE, and Hitachi Energy, and supported adversarial red-team evaluation by Sandia National Laboratories.
  • Spire 2.1: Added reconfiguration capabilities to sustain operation under compound cyber and physical threats.
